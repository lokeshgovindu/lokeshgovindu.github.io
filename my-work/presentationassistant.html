PresentationAssistant

Whenever you run a command in Visual Studio, PresentationAssistant shows its name and every key binding it has, for a few seconds, just above the status bar. It is made for screen sharing, recording a demo, pairing and teaching, where your shortcuts would otherwise fly by unseen. It is just as useful for learning your own key bindings.

Holding a key, or pressing the same shortcut again, shows one line with a count such as x9 instead of flashing nine times. The overlay never takes focus, never appears in Alt+Tab, lets clicks pass through, and hides itself.

  • The default theme is built from your IDE's own colours, and follows a theme switch
  • Nineteen fixed palettes besides, from Classic to Dracula, Nord and High Contrast
  • Your own colours, from a picker or themes.json, with a live preview
  • Excluded commands, with * patterns for the noisy ones
  • Font size, layout, how long it stays, and an option for bound commands only
  • Settings kept as JSON, so they survive upgrades and copy between machines
