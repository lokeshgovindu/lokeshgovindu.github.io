PasteJump

Every clipboard manager can show you a list. The point of PasteJump is that you never have to look at one: hold Ctrl, tap V until the clip you want appears beside the caret, and let go. Your hand never leaves the keyboard and nothing steals focus. The overlay is there so you can look, not because you must.

It reimplements the gesture of Avi Aryan's Clipjump, an AutoHotkey utility last released in 2016, from its observed behaviour, in .NET 10 and WPF, and adds the parts that were missing. A stack of clips holds every clipboard format for the gesture to paste from, and a separate searchable history keeps a record of everything you copied.

  • The overlay appears beside the text caret, or centred on the window you are pasting into
  • Search mid-paste, filter by kind, lock the overlay open, delete a clip
  • Join several clips into a single paste, with a separator of your choosing
  • A history window with search, and import from Clipjump's own store
  • Themes, configurable keys, excluded applications, and a full settings reference
  • Portable: unzip and run, no installer and no registration
