IntelliCommand

Hold Ctrl, Shift or Alt for a moment and it lists every shortcut that starts that way; press the first half of a chord, such as Ctrl+K, and it shows what can follow. The quickest way to find the shortcuts you never knew Visual Studio had.

View on Marketplace About my VS 2022 port

IntelliCommand was written by Den Gladkikh, Aditya Mandaleeka and Drake Campbell, on the Visual Studio team, for Visual Studio 2010 to 2013. This listing is my port of it to Visual Studio 2022.
