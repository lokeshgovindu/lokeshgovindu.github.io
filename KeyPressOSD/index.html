KeyPressOSD help

KeyPressOSD shows what you press, on screen, at a size somebody can read from across a room. It is for screencasts and presentations, for teaching and pair work, and for reading your own typing when the field you are typing into is small or far away.

Ten seconds of it working: typed characters arriving one at a time, a chord, Alt + Tab counting up rather than flickering, the oldest entries scrolling off behind an ellipsis, the fade, and then the same keyboard read as words in typing mode. The same tour is a video if you would rather watch it at full size.

In a hurry

PressWhat happens
Ctrl+Alt+PPause and resume recording. The tray icon is grey while paused.
Ctrl+Alt+KSwitch between keystrokes and typing.
Ctrl+Alt+CClear what is on screen now.
Double-click the tray iconSettings - around fifty of them in nine tabs, with a search box that looks inside the explanations as well as the labels.
Right-click the tray iconThe menu: pause, mode, settings, this manual, and running elevated.

Everything on this site is also in the CHM (KeyPressOSD.chm, or Help in the tray menu), which is built from these same files.
